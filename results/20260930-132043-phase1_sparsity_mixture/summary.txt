           picojoules per byte. It is a DIFFERENCE between two operands,
           not the energy needed to move a byte; that quantity is much
           larger and is not measured anywhere in this project. The
           distinction matters because published DRAM transport figures
           of 10-20 pJ/bit are the other one.
