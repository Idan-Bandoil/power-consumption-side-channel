aborted session -- kept as evidence, not as a measurement
==============================================================================
  Stopped 9 runs into 70. Nothing here is quotable: no condition has its
  repeats, and the instrument was in the state described below for part of it.

  What it caught. util/sampler.c seeded its RAPL period estimate from the
  first observed interval, which is a fragment of a period rather than a
  period -- the sampler opens at an arbitrary phase within one, so that first
  dtsc is uniform in (0, T]. A separate change had moved the EWMA update into
  the else-branch of the overshoot test, so an edge classified as late no
  longer fed the estimator. Those two together make an absorbing state: seed
  under about two thirds of T and every subsequent real edge trips the
  overshoot test, which is also the branch that cannot correct it. The
  estimate froze at the seed for the whole run.

  Five of these nine runs latched, at 0.17-0.23 ms against a true 0.96-0.97 ms,
  and reported 99.99% of their edges late. Recomputed from the recorded
  intervals the same runs read 0.16-0.29% -- so the sampler was recording
  correctly and was only wrong about itself. Recorded energies are unaffected
  either way, because the poll loop runs until the counter actually moves; what
  the estimate drives is the guard window, so a latched run polls about 80% of
  each period instead of 12%, and its overshoot figure means nothing.

  This regression postdates the published corpus. At c0939cb -- the commit the
  tier-1 rate sweep ran at -- the EWMA ran unconditionally on every edge and
  the overshoot test only incremented a counter, so no latch was possible and
  no published number moves.

  Fixed in util/sampler.c by discarding the opening fragment and learning
  unconditionally through warmup, which makes the absorbing state unreachable
  rather than unlikely. analysis/covert.py now recomputes the overshoot
  fraction from the recorded dtsc and fails a run whose sampler disagreed with
  its own trace, so a gate no longer rests on the instrument's opinion of
  itself. tests/test_covert_decode.py pins both.

  The decode below therefore runs on the FIXED analysis against traces taken
  with the BROKEN sampler. The estimator-disagreement failures are the point.

  ties: 195 of 2304 voted bits split evenly across frames and were
  decided on the summed decision margin rather than resolved to 0. An even
  frame count is what makes that possible; a high tie rate is also a symptom,
  since frames only disagree this much when there is nothing for them to agree on.

  BER over payload bits, decoded at the sync the receiver recovered.
  BER|snc is the same decode on the TRUE chip grid, so it measures
  demodulation with acquisition removed; acq says whether the recovered
  sync landed within half a chip. A run with acq=NO has a BER at chance
  whatever the channel did, so read BER and BER|snc together: they part
  company exactly where the failure is acquisition and not noise. |pk| is
  the winning correlation, the acquisition statistic a real receiver could
  compute for itself -- it has no sync column to check. Peak over best
  sidelobe, the obvious alternative, does not separate the two at all.
  pre = preamble BER (a sync check);
  vote = BER after majority vote across frames; dW and sd are
  the per-chip separation and noise, d-marg their ratio. d-pair is
  the same for the within-symbol difference the decision actually
  uses, and Q(pair) is the error rate it predicts -- compare that
  with BER, not d-marg, which ignores noise shared between chips.
  ovr% is the share of RAPL edges seen late; sync = recovered
  start minus true start, in chips.

  acq       : repeats whose recovered sync landed within half a chip.
  BER|snc   : mean BER demodulated on the true chip grid. Where this is
              far below BER, the rate was limited by acquisition and not
              by per-chip noise, and the remedy is a better preamble
              rather than a slower symbol.
  cap b/s   : raw rate x (1 - H(BER)), the BSC capacity at the aggregated
              BER. This is the rate/reliability figure to quote and compare
              against the literature; BER at a rate is not one number.
  vote b/s  : raw / frames, the rate actually delivered after the majority
              vote, since the F frames carry one payload between them.
  bits d/tx : distinct payload bits / times they were transmitted. p<chance
              is a binomial over the second, which assumes every
              transmission is an independent truth. It is not: a decoder
              biased but independent of the message errs the same way on
              every repeat of a bit, so the effective n is the first
              number. Read p as an upper bound on the evidence.
